✶✶✶ Formula

For parallax measured in arcseconds, distance in parsecs is:

d (pc) = 1 / p (arcseconds)

Since 1 arcsecond = 1000 milliarcseconds, this calculator uses:

d (pc) = 1000 / p (mas)

This simple relation assumes the measured parallax is positive and sufficiently reliable. Very small or uncertain parallaxes can produce highly uncertain distances.
